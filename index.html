01

Research

Connecting storms, rainfall, and watershed response.

I am an environmental engineer with a master’s degree in atmospheric sciences and a Ph.D. candidate in Civil and Environmental Engineering at the University of Iowa. My work aims to advance quantitative precipitation forecasting for flash-flood prediction by combining storm tracking, satellite precipitation, atmospheric processes, and hydrologic modeling.

01

Precipitation predictability

Evaluating precipitation forcings, nowcasting approaches, and the latency–lead-time tradeoffs that matter for operational flash-flood forecasting.

02

Mesoscale convective systems

Tracking MCS lifecycles and examining how mesoscale-to-synoptic environments influence storm behavior, rainfall, and future change.

03

Flash-flood forecasting

Developing scalable hydrologic simulations and early-warning applications using satellite Earth observations and high-resolution terrain data.
